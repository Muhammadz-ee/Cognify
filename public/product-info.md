# Cognify: The Best AI YouTube Summarizer and Study Tool

## Product Overview

**Cognify** is the premium AI intelligence layer that transforms how you consume educational content. Distill YouTube videos and podcasts into personal Masterclasses with AI-powered summaries, custom Key Moments, intelligent Insights, and interactive Q&A.

## Core Features

### 1. **Key Moments**
Automatically identifies and extracts the most impactful moments from your favorite content. No more scrubbing through long videos—get straight to what matters.

### 2. **AI Insights**
Deep, contextual analysis of podcast episodes and videos powered by advanced language models. Understand themes, connections, and actionable takeaways instantly.

### 3. **NoteVault**
A personal, organized repository for your notes and learning materials. Sync insights across episodes and access your knowledge base anytime.

### 4. **AI-Powered Q&A**
Ask natural language questions about the content you've consumed. Cognify's AI understands context and provides precise, sourced answers.
